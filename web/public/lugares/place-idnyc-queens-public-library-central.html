IDNYC enrollment centerQueensID

This is an official City enrollment center where you can apply for the IDNYC card, a free government-issued photo ID. According to the program, all New Yorkers ages 10 and up qualify.

How they help

  • Staff help you apply for the IDNYC card in person.
  • The IDNYC works for City services, benefits, library cards, and discounts.
  • Walk-ins are welcome at this location on Fridays.
  • All other locations require an appointment; new slots open every Friday.
  • The card is free, according to the program's official information.
