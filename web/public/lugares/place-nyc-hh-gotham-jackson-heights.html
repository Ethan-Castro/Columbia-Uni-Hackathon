¿Quién califica para NYC Care?

Frequently asked questions

What is NYC Care?

It is an affordable health care program at NYC Health + Hospitals for New Yorkers who don't have and can't afford health insurance.

Who qualifies for NYC Care?

According to the listing: live in NYC, have no employer insurance, don't qualify for plans in New York State, and can't afford insurance by government guidelines.
