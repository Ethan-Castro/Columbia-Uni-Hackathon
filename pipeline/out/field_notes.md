# Field notes: Corona phone spot-check (Saturday, 2026-10-10)

Felix called 8 of the 25 demo-area places on the review list (a sample, not all 25). This is a small, non-random spot check, so read it as stories, not statistics.

## What happened

| Result | Places |
|---|---|
| Reached a live person who confirmed the listing | 1 (Bethany Pantry, Elmhurst) |
| Recorded message gave hours different from, or missing in, the listing | 3 (Mt. Olivet Gospel Church; Public Health Solutions WIC, Corona; NYC Health + Hospitals/Elmhurst WIC) |
| Listed phone number wrong or not working | 2 (Iglesia Cristiana Evangélica: wrong number; La Victoria Foundation: number did not work) |
| No answer: closed when called | 2 (Corona Seventh-day Adventist Church; Make the Road NY, Corona office) |

## Points for the pitch

- **Saturday need, weekday answers.** Someone who needs food on a Saturday often can't call to check: Corona SDA's pantry runs Monday mornings, and on Saturday there was no one to ask. Listings have to be right before someone needs them.
- **Listings drift.** Of the 4 places where we got hours by phone, 2 differed from the City's listing and 1 had no hours listed at all. Mt. Olivet's evening slot in the City listing turned out to be Monday-Wednesday 10am-2pm.
- **The detail that matters isn't in any listing.** Bethany Pantry confirmed its Saturday 9-10am hours and said people can call ahead to have a bag prepared.
- **Providers want this.** A pantry director said they appreciated the check, because since the pandemic many of the maps and lists people use don't work or have wrong information. The director estimated around 90% of them; that is their impression, not a measured figure.

## How the data changed

- Bethany Pantry is the first `verified` place (a person confirmed it by phone).
- 3 places got updated hours from their own recorded messages; that updates the listing but does not verify it.
- Iglesia Cristiana's wrong phone number was removed. La Victoria's DOJ-listed number was kept, since it is the official listing, and flagged.
