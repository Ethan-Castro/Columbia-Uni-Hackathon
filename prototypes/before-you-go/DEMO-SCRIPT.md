# 20-second demo script: Antes de ir / Before You Go

(0-4s) "Before someone travels across the city, they should know three things: is the place open, will it take them, and what to bring."

(4-10s) Tap **Comida**, type **11368**, tap **Entre semana por la tarde/noche**, tap **Ver lugares**.
"Three real pantries from our places.csv, with stated hours, the source link, and the date we last checked."

(10-15s) Point at the grey **desconocido** badge and the yellow **Llama primero** box.
"When the source does not say, we say unknown. We never guess that a place is closed or that it speaks Spanish."

(15-20s) Scroll to the **Antes de salir** card, tap **Copiar las preguntas**, tap **English** to show the switch.
"Five questions to ask on the phone, in Spanish or English, ready to paste. No name, no phone number, no status. Nothing stored."
